# The provider — what we sell in Australia

Researched October 2026 from public sources. The BDM should correct anything here that's wrong; internal material beats this.

## Company
- Established managed hosting and private-cloud provider with data centre locations across many regions worldwide; recently expanded into new markets.
- **Australia launch: 2026.** Australian-hosted data centre locations in **Sydney and Melbourne**, both live. Local office with AU sales, support and engineering.
- Launch positioning: Australian organisations wanting cloud infrastructure **hosted under Australian jurisdiction** — data sovereignty, cybersecurity, AI readiness.

## Services
- Private Cloud (single-tenant, dedicated) and VPC (isolated multi-tenant)
- Enterprise Cloud, dedicated and bare-metal servers
- Managed Azure and Managed AWS (the provider runs the public cloud for you)
- Managed Kubernetes / OpenShift
- Security services, colocation, virtual desktops, SFTP, managed WordPress

## Differentiators (the reasons a customer switches)
- **Direct-to-engineer 24/7/365 support**, urgent response under 20 minutes — no tier-1 ticket queue. Strong for mid-market firms with a 2–5 person IT team who can't staff nights/weekends.
- **Bespoke design** by engineers rather than one-size-fits-all.
- **Fixed monthly billing**, no surprise fees, proactive right-sizing — a pitch against unpredictable hyperscaler bills.
- 100% network uptime SLA, 99.99% cloud server uptime, 20-minute hardware replacement.
- Partner ecosystem across major data centre, hardware, networking and virtualisation vendors.
- Channel: works with partners (referral, co-sell, or white-label).

## Reference customers (global)
- Secure healthcare platforms with 24/7 support.
- High-performance eCommerce infrastructure built for traffic spikes.
- Sectors served: eCommerce, financial services, healthcare, AI/ML, education, blockchain, digital agencies.

## Talk tracks by pain
| Pain the prospect has | Angle |
|---|---|
| VMware/Broadcom licence renewal shock | Move VMs to managed private cloud/VPC; the provider carries the platform licensing and ops |
| Ageing on-prem servers or comms room, hardware refresh due | Avoid capex; migrate to managed private cloud in Australian SYD/MEL data centres |
| Data must stay in Australia (health, finance, legal, education, gov suppliers) | Australian-hosted, Australian jurisdiction |
| Small IT team, no after-hours cover | Direct-to-engineer 24/7 support acts as an extension of their team |
| AWS/Azure bill blowing out or hard to manage | Managed AWS/Azure, or repatriate steady workloads to fixed-price private cloud |
| Post-breach / Essential Eight / board cyber pressure | Managed security services on hardened infrastructure |
| eCommerce peaks (EOFY, Black Friday, Click Frenzy) | Proven high-performance eCommerce hosting |
